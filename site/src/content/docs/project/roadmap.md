---
title: Roadmap
---

The roadmap covers 12 months, split into seven phases.

| Phase | Weeks | Content | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0: Foundation | 1–2 | Repository, licenses, devcontainer, CI, Starlight site, curriculum | In progress |
| P1: Robot Body | 3–8 | ROS 2 basics, URDF/xacro, TF, sensors, Gazebo world | Planned |
| P2: Robot Brain: Navigation | 9–16 | Odometry, SLAM, AMCL, Nav2, behavior trees | Planned |
| P3: Robot Eyes: Perception | 17–24 | Camera on GPU cloud, OpenCV, detection, depth, sensor fusion | Planned |
| P4: Data & Evaluation | 25–32 | rosbag, metadata, benchmarks, failure analysis | Planned |
| P5: Learning-based Physical AI | 33–44 | Imitation learning, RL, VLA, sim-to-real (LeRobot, MuJoCo Playground) | Planned, depends on GPU |
| P6: Edge & Real Robot (optional) | 45–52 | Jetson or a low-cost robot | Planned, depends on hardware |

:::note
P1–P4 stand on their own as a complete project. P5 and P6 depend on GPU and hardware, and the project does not need them to have value.
:::
